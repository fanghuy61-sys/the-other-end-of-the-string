The story

When the bells chose his steps,
his colours faded.

Once, the stage lights knew every shade of this puppet’s wood. Then the bells began telling him when to move and where to stand. By the night of the final performance, even his name was missing from the programme.

Guide him backstage before the String Collector finds him. A clock can hold a bridge still. A folded page can become a path. Each time he makes a choice of his own, another shade returns. What he writes on the last line is up to you.
